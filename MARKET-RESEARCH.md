# Epic Arts — demand research & catalog strategy

**Research reviewed: October 24, 2026.** Heritage-first digital art, with adjacent contemporary/interiors directions.

## Executive decision

Build a recognizable visual world, not an undifferentiated pile of AI images. Keep Mithila-inspired nature, folk pattern and radial geometry at the center. Add expressive modern geometry, coordinated botanical studies, and warm abstraction so shoppers can build a personal gallery wall.

The implemented catalog contains **1,024 algorithmic artworks across 16 families**, plus the **8 original products** (six single artworks and two sets): **1,032 public purchasable digital editions**. Each generative family has 64 reproducible compositions/colorways across eight palettes. These are not 1,024 hand-painted originals or 1,024 separate artistic traditions.

The studio creates additional unlisted editions on demand. A buyer can select a family, palette, detail level and seed, or use “surprise me,” then collect the result through the existing checkout and protected download library. Its built-in engine is procedural design, **not arbitrary text-to-image AI**. Mood matching is explicitly labeled simple keyword matching. The optional live image-provider path is disabled until configured.

### Important counter-signal

The newer Etsy report’s **“Mark of the Maker”** emphasizes visible human craft, brushstrokes and stitching. Generative digital editions should **not** be positioned as a replacement for authenticated artisan-made work. Epic Arts discloses its methods, does not claim an artisan partnership, and encourages customers seeking authentic hand-painted art to buy from practicing artists. [1](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1521255894814)

## Merchandising implemented

- Heritage remains prominent in the homepage, original edit, story and studio families.
- `/collections` introduces all 16 families with actual artwork previews.
- `/trending` separates research-led editorial selection from measured shop popularity and shows source dates and methodology.
- Gallery pages return **24 products**, with indexed search, filters and pagination rather than 1,000 cards in the DOM.
- New generated editions begin with **zero reviews and no fabricated rating**.
- Paid-purchase and buyer-rating sorts read paid-order data only. Demo orders and seeded reviews do not boost those rankings. A cold-start message is shown when there are no paid orders.
- Clear source-quality distinctions: native-vector engine work, original upscaled AI-assisted art, and optional raster image-AI work.
- A real generative download contains SVG, PNG, JPG, three artwork PDFs, recipe JSON, a print guide and a personal-use license.

## What has NOT been measured

- No authenticated Etsy Marketplace Insights, Pinterest account analytics, Google Trends account analysis, Search Console or Keyword Planner data was available.
- No trustworthy absolute monthly volume, geographic demand estimate, conversion rate, traffic forecast, revenue forecast, or bestseller claim can be made from this research.
- No external trend ingestion scheduler is running. This is a manually reviewed research edit, not a live demand feed.
- This installation’s demo orders do not establish commercial product-market fit.

## Next commercial validation steps

1. Connect the real domain to Search Console and submit the sitemap. Inspect actual search queries and landing pages before expanding paid acquisition.
2. If selling on Etsy, validate candidate phrases in the operator’s Marketplace Insights account, in the intended category and target market. Do not scrape unsupported “monthly volume” claims into product badges.
3. Test a small selection of coherent families first. Compare product-view → saved-piece → checkout → **paid order** → successful-download conversion, with consent-appropriate first-party measurement.
4. Review results by family, palette and price. Separate paid, demo, refunded and disputed orders; avoid encouraging low-quality duplication just to increase product count.
5. Use an editorial review gate before publishing new AI-provider outputs. Review rights, cultural presentation, print quality and duplicates. Current visitor-created studio products are deliberately unlisted, not automatically dumped into the public catalog.
6. Revisit this research monthly. Keep observation windows and publication dates visible; never relabel an old forecast as live demand.

Epic Arts now has a real, usable catalog and creation pipeline; the next step is commercial validation with actual buyers.
