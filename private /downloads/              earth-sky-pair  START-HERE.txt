Your art collection

Each artwork is in its own ZIP folder. Unzip each one to access the JPG, three print-ready PDFs, print guide, and personal-use license.

A LITTLE GUIDE TO YOUR NEW ART

1. Download and unzip the folder on your computer or phone.
2. Choose an included PDF: 5 x 7 inches, 8 x 10 inches, or A4 (with a white border).
3. Print at 100% / actual size. Disable 'fit to page' and automatic color enhancement.
4. We recommend a matte fine-art or textured paper of 200–300 gsm, with a test print first.
5. Slip it into a favorite frame and make it yours.

FILES & IMAGE QUALITY
The JPG is 2400 x 3000 pixels with 300 DPI metadata, upscaled from an AI-assisted
1122 x 1402 pixel source. DPI metadata is not native detail. For best results,
we recommend printing at 8 x 10 inches or smaller. A4 includes generous margins.
The original source proportions are preserved. Screen and print colors may differ.
No physical artwork, frame, or shipping is included.

Keep a backup of your downloaded folder. Enjoy your little piece of heritage.
